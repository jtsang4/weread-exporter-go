# Code of Conduct

This project and everyone who takes part in it are expected to be respectful. 请友善、尊重地交流。

## Our standard

Welcome:

- polite, patient and constructive discussion, in Chinese or English;
- giving and accepting feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- admitting mistakes and learning from them.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination of any kind;
- trolling, spam, or publishing someone's private information (including accounts, cookies and book contents) without their permission;
- asking for, or offering, ways to get around WeRead's access rules or to use other people's accounts;
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and to people who represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban people for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report problems to the maintainer [@jtsang4](https://github.com/jtsang4) on GitHub (use a private channel for sensitive matters, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); all reports are handled with discretion and without retaliation.
